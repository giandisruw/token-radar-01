Which launch is worth farming next?

Every protocol below records no token — the snapshot lists no CoinGecko id, no symbol and no market cap for it. They are ranked by the implied value their token would carry if their record stayed as it is. That value is this entry’s own claim, never a prediction that a token will launch, or when.

How the value is worked out — model radar-3

implied = TVL × anchor × growth × maturity × breadth

The anchor is the market talking, not a hand-picked number. For each category the model takes the snapshot’s own protocols whose token already trades, and uses the median of their market-cap-to-TVL ratios as what one dollar of TVL is worth in that line of business. A category with fewer than three traded peers has no anchor of its own and falls back to the snapshot-wide median. The table below prints every anchor with its peer count, so any reader can check the calibration.

  • anchor — category median mcap/TVL over traded peers (3+ peers), else the snapshot-wide median.
  • growth — 1 + 0.6 × 7-day TVL change, change clamped to −50% … +100% (no change reported counts as 0).
  • maturity — 0.50 + 0.50 × √(age in days, capped at 1,825) / 1,825. The first year of survival moves the needle most; no listing date counts as the snapshot’s median age.
  • breadth — 1 + 0.05 × log₂(chains). The second chain matters more than the tenth.
