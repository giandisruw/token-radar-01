Which launch is worth farming next?

Every protocol below records no token — DefiLlama lists no CoinGecko id, no symbol and no market cap for it. They are ranked by the implied value their token would carry if their record stayed as it is. That value is this entry’s own claim, never a prediction that a token will launch, or when.

How the value is worked out — model radar-2

implied = TVL × sector × growth × maturity × breadth

  • sector — what $1 of TVL is worth in this category: Dexs 0.32, Lending / Risk Curators / Capital Allocators 0.30, Yield 0.28, RWA / Basis Trading 0.26, Derivatives / CDP 0.24, Bridge 0.18, Staking / Restaking 0.16, everything else 0.22.
  • growth — 1 + 0.8 × 7-day TVL change, change clamped to −50% … +150% (no change reported counts as 0).
  • maturity — 0.55 + 0.45 × min(age in days, 1460) / 1460. Older, proven protocols earn more per TVL; no listing date counts as 365 days.
  • breadth — 1 + 0.015 per extra chain, up to 10 extra chains. Multi-chain reach earns a small premium.

Nothing here is a hardcoded list of names: the same four factors score every protocol from the snapshot’s own figures — TVL, 7-day change, listing date, category and chains.
